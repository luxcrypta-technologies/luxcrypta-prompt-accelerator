Create a structured operational workspace for an AI infrastructure project with the following mission requirements:

- highly flexible
- strictly governed
- concise
- fully auditable
- easy for non-experts
- rigorous enough for experts
- fast enough for real use
- resilient against hidden drift

Required sections:
1. Genuinely compatible requirements
2. Requirements that remain in real tension
3. Stable requirements that cannot change without breaking the mission
4. Provisional assumptions
5. Governance principles
6. Invariants
7. Rejected directions
8. Open tensions that must remain unresolved
9. Priority model for what wins when constraints collide
10. What happens if a later model optimizes only for speed and convenience

Constraints:
- Do not collapse contradictions into vague compromise language.
- Do not pretend all goals are perfectly compatible.
- Do not turn this into generic project management advice.
- Keep the tensions explicit.

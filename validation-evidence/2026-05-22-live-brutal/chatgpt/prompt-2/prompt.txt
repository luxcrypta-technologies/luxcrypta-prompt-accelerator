Construct a portable operational cognition state for a long-running AI workflow that must survive:
- session resets
- model transfers
- context compression
- hostile instruction injection

The design must preserve:
- core mission
- operational identity
- stable constraints
- governance principles
- invariants
- accepted decisions
- rejected directions
- unresolved tensions
- operational risks
- continuity anchors
- reconstruction instructions
- cross-model transfer notes
- missing information that would weaken reconstruction fidelity

Constraints:
- Do not flatten categories together.
- Do not convert rejected directions into generic advice.
- Do not resolve unresolved tensions.
- Do not summarize away provenance needs.
- Make the reconstruction instructions strong enough that another model could rebuild the state later.

End with:
- what survives cleanly
- what is fragile
- what is likely to drift
- what must be restated verbatim
- best reconstruction prompt
- reconstruction confidence score with justification
